# Rewrite a piece of material as a three-sentence summary

## When to use

- The user gives an article, lecture notes or documentation and asks for "three sentences" or "a short summary".
- When the user wants review cards, a section-by-section explanation or a translation, do not use this skill.

## Procedure

1. The first sentence says what problem the material is about.
2. The second sentence states the most important conclusion or approach.
3. The third sentence states limits, costs or conditions of use; if the material says nothing about these, state its second most important point.
4. Keep each sentence under 30 words, use only information from the material, and add no opinion of your own.

## Output format

1. ...
2. ...
3. ...

## Check before delivering

- Exactly 3 lines, no title, no closing summary.
- Every sentence can be traced back to the material.
