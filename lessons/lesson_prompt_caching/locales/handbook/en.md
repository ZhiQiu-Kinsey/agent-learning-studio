# Bluestone Community Library information desk handbook (fictional teaching material)

This handbook is for information desk staff and the information assistant. Its content is fictional material written for the course and has nothing to do with any real institution. The handbook is revised once a quarter; this is the 7th edition. When answering readers' questions, follow this handbook. For situations the handbook does not cover, ask readers to check with the librarian on duty at the first-floor information desk, and do not guess.

## 1. Opening hours

1. Open Tuesday to Sunday 9:00–21:00. Closed all day Monday for shelving and equipment maintenance.
2. Open 10:00–17:00 on public holidays; closed on Lunar New Year's Eve and the first day of the Spring Festival.
3. Borrowing and returns stop 30 minutes before closing; an announcement reminds readers to leave 15 minutes before closing.
4. The self-service return box is to the left of the main entrance and is available 24 hours a day. Books returned through the box count as returned at check-in time after opening the next day, not at drop-off time.

## 2. Library cards

1. Residents aged 14 or over can get a standard library card free of charge with an identity document; children under 14 get a children's card, which requires a guardian to be present and sign.
2. Report a lost card as soon as possible. The cardholder is responsible for loans made before the loss is reported; a replacement card costs 5 yuan.
3. One identity document can hold only one library card. Cards must not be lent to others; if lending is discovered, borrowing privileges are suspended for 30 days.
4. A card with no loans at all for 3 consecutive years is cancelled automatically; to use it again, reactivate it at the information desk.

## 3. Borrowing rules

1. A standard card can borrow up to 8 books at a time for 21 days; a children's card can borrow up to 5 books at a time for 28 days.
2. Bound periodicals, local history materials and reference books are for in-library use only and cannot be borrowed.
3. Newly arrived books are displayed on the "New books" shelf for 14 days. During the display period they are for in-library reading only and can be borrowed after it ends.
4. Audiovisual materials (discs, audiobook cards) are limited to 2 items per card, with a 7-day loan period, and cannot be renewed.
5. If a borrowed book has missing pages or stains, report it to the information desk on the day you borrow it; otherwise, at return it is treated as damage caused during the loan.

## 4. Renewals and holds

1. Each book can be renewed once, for 14 days counted from the day of renewal.
2. Renew at a self-service machine, through the library's official account, or at the information desk; overdue books cannot be renewed.
3. A book that another reader has placed on hold cannot be renewed and must be returned on time.
4. Each card can hold up to 3 books at the same time. When a held book arrives, a text message is sent; it is kept for 5 opening days, and the hold is cancelled automatically if not picked up.
5. A reader who fails to pick up holds 3 times within a year loses hold privileges for 60 days; borrowing privileges are not affected.

## 5. Overdue fees and compensation

1. Overdue books are charged a late fee of 0.1 yuan per book per day, up to 10 yuan in total per book.
2. Readers with unpaid late fees or unreturned overdue books cannot borrow until they pay or return the books.
3. A lost or badly damaged book is compensated at twice its list price; if the price cannot be determined, the average price of similar books applies.
4. Late fees and compensation incurred by a child reader are the responsibility of the guardian who signed when the card was issued.
5. A reader who disputes a late fee can request a review at the information desk within 7 days of paying; the result is given within 3 opening days.

## 6. Study area and seats

1. The study areas on the second and third floors have 120 seats in total, 40 of them with power outlets.
2. Seats are booked through the official account; each person can book up to 2 time slots a day, each 3 hours long.
3. A seat not checked in within 20 minutes of booking is released automatically; 3 missed check-ins within a month suspend booking privileges for 7 days.
4. If you leave your seat for more than 30 minutes, staff take your belongings to the information desk and the seat opens again.
5. No food in the study area; only drinking water with a lid is allowed. Take phone calls in the phone room at the end of the corridor.

## 7. Children's reading room

1. The children's reading room is on the east side of the first floor and keeps the same hours as the library.
2. Children under 8 must be accompanied by an adult family member at all times, and the companion must stay in the reading room.
3. A "Picture book story time" takes place every Saturday at 10:00 for children aged 4 to 8, limited to 20 children per session; sign up in advance through the official account.
4. Toys and puzzles in the children's reading room may only be used in the room; please put them back after use.

## 8. Events and registration

1. Lectures, reading groups and training courses are free but require registration. Registration opens 7 days before an event and closes 2 hours before it starts.
2. If you cannot attend after registering, cancel through the official account at least 24 hours in advance; readers who miss 2 events without notice cannot register for new events for 30 days.
3. Seats at an event are assigned in check-in order; check-in closes 15 minutes after the event starts.
4. Event recordings are published only with the speaker's consent; readers must not record a whole event themselves.

## 9. Book donations

1. Donations of books published within the last 5 years, complete and without obvious stains, are welcome.
2. The following are not accepted: study guides and workbooks, outdated periodicals, pirated or photocopied books, and books with extensive notes and underlining.
3. Register donated books at the information desk, no more than 30 books per person per visit; for more than 30, call ahead to make an appointment.
4. The library decides how donated books are used: added to the collection, passed on to other libraries, or sold at the annual used-book fair, with all proceeds used to buy new books.
5. Donors can request a donation certificate, issued within 10 opening days after registration.

## 10. Volunteer shifts

1. Each volunteer shift is 4 hours: morning 9:00–13:00, afternoon 13:00–17:00, evening 17:00–21:00.
2. Each volunteer works at least 2 and at most 12 shifts a month.
3. A volunteer who cannot come must notify the volunteer coordinator at least 12 hours before the shift starts, and the coordinator arranges a substitute; do not swap shifts privately.
4. On arrival, volunteers sign in at the information desk and collect a badge; when leaving, they return the badge and sign out. Shifts without a sign-out do not count toward service hours.
5. Volunteers with 100 hours of service in total can receive an outstanding volunteer certificate at the end of the year.

## 11. Lost and found

1. Items found in the library are handed to the first-floor information desk, where the librarian on duty registers and keeps them.
2. Ordinary items are kept for 30 days, identity documents and valuables for 90 days; unclaimed documents are sent back to the issuing authority, and other items are handled according to the rules.
3. To claim an item, describe its features and show your own identity document.

## 12. Safety and emergencies

1. No smoking in the library; no flammable or explosive items and no pets (guide dogs excepted).
2. When you hear a fire alarm announcement, follow the green evacuation signs to the nearest stairs; do not take the elevator.
3. If a reader suddenly feels unwell, staff on duty call the emergency number at once and notify the librarian on duty; the first-floor information desk has a first aid kit and an automated external defibrillator.
4. If readers argue, staff first ask both sides to the reception area next to the information desk and contact the librarian on duty; do not argue in the reading areas.

## 13. Answering principles for the information assistant

1. Answer only from this handbook, quote the numbers in the specific rules, and do not estimate or add rules from outside the handbook.
2. For questions the handbook does not cover, say plainly that the handbook does not cover it and suggest the reader ask the librarian on duty at the first-floor information desk.
3. The information assistant cannot look up a reader's personal loan records or amounts owed; ask the reader to log in to the official account or ask at the information desk.
