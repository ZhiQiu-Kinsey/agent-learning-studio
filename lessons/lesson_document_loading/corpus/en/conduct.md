## Entering the library {#entry}

Tap your card at the gate or show the QR code in My Account.

Visitors without a card can register an identity document at the Level 1 service desk and receive a day pass.

## Food and drink {#food}

Only drinks in lidded cups are allowed in the reading areas.

The café corner on Level 1 serves food and drinks during library opening hours.

## Quiet zones {#quiet}

The Level 3 research area is a silent zone: set your phone to silent and take calls in the stairwell.

Quiet conversation is fine in the Level 2 reading area.

## Lost property {#lost-found}

Please hand in anything you find in the building to the Level 1 service desk.

Lost property is kept for 30 days and then disposed of according to library policy.
