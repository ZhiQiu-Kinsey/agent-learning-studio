## How many items {#borrow-limits}

How many items you can borrow at the same time depends on your card:

- A1 standard card: up to 5 items.
- B2 research card: up to 15 items, including no more than 3 back issues of journals.
- C1 children's card: up to 3 items, from the children's reading room only.

Special collections, reference works and the current month's journals are for use in the building only.

## Loan period {#loan-period}

Books are lent for 28 days and back issues of journals for 7 days.

The due date is printed on your checkout slip and shown on the My Account page.

Three days before the due date, we send a reminder to your registered mobile number.

## Extending a loan {#extend-loan}

Each item can be extended once before it is due, for another 14 days.

You can do this at a kiosk, at the service desk or on the My Account page.

Items that another reader has placed on hold cannot be extended, and overdue items must be paid off first.

## Placing holds {#holds}

When every copy of a title is out, you can place a hold on the My Account page; each card can have up to 5 holds at a time.

Holds are queued in the order they are placed, and one copy is set aside for you when your turn comes.

When a reserved item arrives, we send you a text message.

## Pickup deadline {#pickup}

After the notice goes out, it is kept on the pickup shelf beside the Level 1 service desk for 5 opening days.

If nobody comes for it by then, it passes to the next reader in the queue.

After three notices in a row go uncollected, this service is suspended for 60 days.

## Returning items {#returns}

Items can be returned at the service desk on any level, or at the self-service return machine in the Level 1 lobby.

Outside opening hours, put items in the return box to the right of the main entrance; it is emptied when the library opens the next morning.

Keep your return receipt until your account record has been updated.
