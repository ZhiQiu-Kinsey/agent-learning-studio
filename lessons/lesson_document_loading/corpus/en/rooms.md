## Study rooms at a glance {#room-list}

- R-201 to R-204: Level 2, 4 seats each, open to every cardholder.
- R-301 to R-303: Level 3, 8 seats each, B2 research cards only.
- R-305: Level 3, 10 seats, with video conferencing equipment, B2 research cards only.

## How to book {#booking}

Study rooms are booked on the My Account page, up to 7 days ahead and for at most 2 hours at a time.

Each reader can make one booking per day and cannot hold two bookings for the same slot.

Once your study room booking is confirmed you get a text message; then follow the check-in rules below.

## Check-in and release {#check-in}

When you arrive, tap your card on the tablet by the door to check in.

If nobody has checked in 15 minutes after the start time, the space is released to other readers and a no-show is recorded.

Three no-shows within a month suspend this service for a month.

## Borrowing equipment {#equipment}

Every space has a whiteboard and power sockets; projectors, presentation clickers and adapters can be borrowed with your card from the service desk on the same level.

Borrowed equipment must be handed back when your session ends; damage is handled under Charges and Compensation.

## House rules {#room-rules}

Discussion is welcome, but keep the door shut and your voices down.

Only drinks with a lid are allowed inside, and no food.
