## Weekdays {#weekdays}

Monday to Friday: 8:30–21:00.

Every Wednesday from 8:30 to 10:00 the building is tidied and only the Level 1 lobby is open.

## Weekends {#weekend}

Saturday and Sunday: 9:00–18:00.

Card revalidation is not available at weekends; all other services run as usual.

## Hours by level {#floor-hours}

The Level 1 lobby and service desk keep the library's full hours.

The children's reading room on Level 1 shuts at 17:30 on weekdays and opens 9:00–18:00 at weekends.

The Level 3 research area shuts at 20:00 on weekdays, and with the rest of the building at weekends.

## Public holidays {#holidays}

During the Spring Festival the library is shut from New Year's Eve to the second day of the new year and reopens on the third day.

On other public holidays it opens 9:00–17:00; check the website notices for details.

## About this version {#changes}

This version takes effect on 1 March 2026 and replaces the 2025 opening hours.

Main changes: weekdays now run to 21:00, and both weekend days are 9:00–18:00.
