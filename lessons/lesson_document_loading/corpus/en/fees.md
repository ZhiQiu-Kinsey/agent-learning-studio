## Overdue charges {#overdue}

Items brought back after their due date are charged 0.5 yuan per item per day.

The overdue charge for any single item is capped at 20 yuan.

C1 children's cards are never charged for overdue items.

## Lost and damaged items {#lost-damaged}

A lost item is paid for at twice its list price; if the price is unknown, the average price of similar items is used.

Stained items or items with missing pages that can still be read incur a 10-yuan repair charge; items that can no longer circulate are treated as lost.

If you find an item after paying for it, the compensation is refunded within 30 days on presentation of the receipt; overdue charges are not refunded.

## Paying charges {#payment}

All charges can be settled at the service desk or a kiosk, using account balance, debit or credit cards, or a mobile wallet.

Keep the printed receipt until the end of the year; you will need it for enquiries and refunds.

## Unpaid charges and blocked accounts {#account-block}

Once unpaid charges on an account exceed 10 yuan, borrowing, holds and loan extensions are all blocked.

The block is lifted as soon as the charges are settled; no separate request is needed.
