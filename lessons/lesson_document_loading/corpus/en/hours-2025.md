## Weekdays {#weekdays}

Monday to Friday: 9:00–20:30.

Every Wednesday morning the building is shut for tidying and opens at 12:00.

## Weekends {#weekend}

Saturday 9:00–17:00; Sunday 10:00–17:00.

## Hours by level {#floor-hours}

The children's reading room on Level 1 shuts at 17:00 on weekdays.

The Level 3 research area shuts at 19:30 on weekdays and stays shut at weekends.

## Public holidays {#holidays}

During the Spring Festival the library is shut from New Year's Eve to the third day of the new year.

On other public holidays it opens 9:00–16:00.
