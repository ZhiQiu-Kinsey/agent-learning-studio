## Level 1 {#level-1}

Level 1 has the service desk, self-service checkout machines, the children's reading room and the café corner.

The pickup shelf is next to the service desk, which also takes in lost property.

## Level 2 {#level-2}

Level 2 holds the Chinese and foreign-language book collection, shelved by subject.

The east side of Level 2 has four small study rooms and a self-service printer and copier.

## Level 3 {#level-3}

Level 3 is the research area, with the special collections reading room, the journals section and four study rooms.

Special collections can only be read inside the special collections reading room, and entry requires a B2 research card.

## Accessibility {#accessibility}

There is a ramp at the main entrance and an accessible toilet on every level.

The lift on the north side of the lobby serves every level, and wheelchairs can be borrowed at the service desk.
