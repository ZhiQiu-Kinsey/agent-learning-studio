## Wi-Fi {#wifi}

The in-building wireless network is BLUESTONE-READER; sign in with your card number and account password.

Each sign-in lasts up to 4 hours; when it runs out, simply sign in again.

Visitors with a day pass use BLUESTONE-GUEST, limited to 2 hours a day.

## Off-site database access {#remote-access}

A1 and B2 readers can reach the online databases from outside the library through My Account; C1 children's cards do not include this.

Each account can be signed in on two devices at a time.

## E-books {#ebooks}

Each card can borrow up to 10 e-books a month for 21 days each; they expire automatically and never run up overdue charges.

E-book loans cannot be extended, but you can borrow the title again once it expires.

## Printing and copying {#printing}

Levels 2 and 3 each have a self-service printer and copier, paid for from your card account balance.

Black and white costs 0.2 yuan per page; colour costs 1 yuan per page.

Scanning is free and can be sent to your registered email address.
