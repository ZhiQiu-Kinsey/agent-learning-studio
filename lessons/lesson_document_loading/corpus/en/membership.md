## Card types {#card-types}

The library issues three kinds of card:

- A1 standard card: for residents of the city aged 14 or over.
- B2 research card: for university teachers, graduate students and registered researchers in the city.
- C1 children's card: for readers under 14, issued with a parent or guardian present.

How many items each card can borrow is set out in the Borrowing Rules.

## What to bring {#apply}

For an A1 or C1 card, bring an original identity document; for C1, also bring the guardian's identity document.

For a B2 card, bring an identity document plus a letter from your institution confirming that you work or study there.

Cards are free and are issued on the spot when the documents are complete.

## Lost cards and replacements {#replacement}

If you lose your card, report it on the My Account page or at the service desk as soon as possible; once reported, nobody else can borrow with it.

A replacement card costs 10 yuan; the old card stops working and your borrowing history is kept.

## Validity and annual renewal {#validity}

A1 and C1 cards do not expire.

B2 research cards must be revalidated every September with a new letter from your institution.

A B2 card that is not revalidated in time becomes an A1 standard card, with the lower borrowing limit.
